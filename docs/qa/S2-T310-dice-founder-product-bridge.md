# S2-T310 Dice Founder product bridge

The bridge now targets the existing Dice result card. It supplies loading,
structured interpretation, safety, fallback, and retry states without changing
the three landed-symbol cells, throw animation, navigation, or existing actions.

The interpretation region has its own bounded scroll area. The symbols and
**Roll again** / **Reflect in Chat** actions remain outside that scroll owner.
Chat navigation occurs only through the existing explicit **Reflect in Chat**
tap and preserves its established draft.

The adapter remains default-off. Without both switches and accepted authority,
no gateway transport is constructed. Founder previews are externally labelled,
zero-effect local fixtures.
